A message queue lets you say "I'll do that in the background, here's your response now."

Producer -> The thing that creates tasks (your API route)
Queue -> Where tasks wait to be processed (Redis or Kafka)
Consumer/Worker -> The thing that picks up and processes tasks
Broker -> The middleman managing the queue (Redis in this case)

Celery is Python's most popular task queue library. It uses Redis as its broker:
